Vessel Overview

Built for Deep Ocean Passage Making: Selene 60 Ocean Explorer

MV ChristieAnn is a custom-outfitted Selene 60 Ocean Explorer trawler designed specifically for long-range transoceanic cruising. With her displacement hull, bulbous bow, and twin engines, she provides non-stop range, exceptional fuel efficiency, and remarkable sea-keeping comfort.

Equipped with redundant navigation systems, satellite communication, watermakers, and active fin stabilization, MV ChristieAnn serves as our self-sustaining mobile home across the globe.
